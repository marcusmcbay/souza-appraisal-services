Souza Appraisal Services

specializes in the valuation of commercial and agricultural properties, including properties subject to eminent domain. Market value appraisals for lending purposes, asset management and estate settlement represent a major component of the company’s work.

The firm is able to meet the needs of government agencies requiring appraisal of eminent domain properties as well as the needs of lenders, attorneys and private parties with individual property valuation requirements.

  • Mark Souza is the principal and senior appraiser with associate membership in the Appraisal Institute and the American Society of Farm Managers and Rural Appraisers.
  • Associate appraisers are experienced, licensed and well-qualified.
