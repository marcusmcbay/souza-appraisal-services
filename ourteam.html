Mark Souza

General Certified Appraiser, CA

Introduction

Mr. Souza is the principal of Souza Appraisal Services. Souza Appraisal Services is a firm that appraises and consults on a wide range of property types for government agencies involved in eminent domain, for collateral valuation for lenders, and for property owners and investors.

Property types appraised include agricultural properties such as orchards, vineyards, open cropland, agribusiness facilities such as dairies. Commercial properties include industrial, office, retail, multi-family, and land properties.

Mr. Souza has nearly 20 years of valuation experience. In 2007 Mr. Souza began his career in real estate as an associate appraiser with K. R. McBay Appraisal Company. Mr. Souza has gained experience in the valuation of a wide range of commercial and agricultural properties.

Specific property types have included office, retail, industrial, multi-family residential, rural residential, commercial and residential land; as well as mixed-use and special-purpose properties.

Special-purpose properties have included religious facilities, walnut huller/dryer facilities, aqua-culture facilities, dairy facilities and RV/mini storage facilities.

Agricultural properties have included orchards, vineyards, open cropland, pastureland, rangeland, agricultural processing facilities, including cold storage facilities. Mr. Souza has broad experience in the valuation of properties affected by eminent domain.

Melody Bold

General Certified Appraiser, CA

Introduction

In 2014, Melody Bold was hired by the K. R. McBay Company to serve as an administrative assistant.

Showing an interest in appraisal work, and under the supervision of Mr. Kenneth McBay, she began to take on a greater role in researching various aspects of properties being appraised by the company as well as reviewing the reports.

In 2017 Mrs. Bold began the coursework and practical experience required to earn her general appraisal certification, working closely with both Mr. McBay and Mr. Souza, and achieved her general certification in 2024.

Melody has appraised a variety of property types including agricultural, industrial, commercial, religious facilities, and multi-family residential uses.
